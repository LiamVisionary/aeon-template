# This is a mirror of AEON

This repository is a copy of **[aeonfun/aeon](https://github.com/aeonfun/aeon)**, which is
open source under the MIT licence. The original licence and copyright are kept intact in
`LICENSE`; upstream's history is preserved here commit for commit.

It exists for one reason. `hivemindos.app/chat` creates each person's automation engine by
copying this template into their own GitHub. The `aeonfun` organisation has **OAuth App access
restrictions** enabled, which is its own good call — but it means a third-party OAuth App
cannot copy a repository owned by that organisation until an `aeonfun` owner approves the app,
and that approval is not ours to grant. Copying from a template we own removes that dependency
for everyone signing in.

Nothing here is a fork in spirit. AEON's authors wrote all of it; if you want the real thing,
or want to contribute, go upstream.

Kept in step with `scripts/sync-aeon-template.sh` in the hivemindos-website repository.
